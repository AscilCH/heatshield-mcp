# WHO Heatwave Emergency Medical Protocol

## Assessment of Heat Stroke
If the patient exhibits confusion, altered mental state, slurred speech, or a core body temperature exceeding 40°C (104°F), they are likely experiencing Heat Stroke. This is a medical emergency. 

## Action Plan
1. **Immediate Cooling**: Move the patient to an air-conditioned environment. Remove outer clothing.
2. **Cold Water Immersion**: The most effective way to lower core temperature is immersing the patient in an ice-water bath. If unavailable, apply ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
3. **DO NOT GIVE FLUIDS**: If the patient is confused or vomiting, do not force them to drink liquids due to the choking hazard.
4. **Call Emergency Services**: Dial 112 (Europe) or 911 (US) immediately.

## Assessment of Heat Exhaustion
Symptoms include heavy sweating, cold, pale, and clammy skin, fast weak pulse, nausea or vomiting, muscle cramps, tiredness or weakness.

## Action Plan
1. Move to a cool place.
2. Loosen clothes.
3. Put cool, wet cloths on the body or take a cool bath.
4. Sip water. 
5. Get medical help right away if vomiting continues or symptoms last longer than 1 hour.
